## 1. Tiempos verbales

### 1.1 Present Simple
**Uso:** rutinas, hechos permanentes, verdades, horarios. Clave: *always, usually, often, never, every day*.

- 3ª persona singular: verbo + **-s/-es** → *she plays, it restarts*
- Negativa: don't / doesn't + infinitivo
- Interrogativa: Do / Does + sujeto + infinitivo?

> *My brother plays video games on weekends.*
> *The server usually restarts at midnight.*

---

### 1.2 Present Continuous
**Uso:** acción en curso ahora, planes cercanos confirmados. Clave: *now, at the moment, right now, look!*

- Estructura: am/is/are + verbo-**ing**

> *Look, the students are working on a project right now.*
> *I can't talk now, I am cooking dinner.*

---

### 1.3 Present Perfect Simple
**Uso:** acción pasada con resultado en el presente, experiencias, estado que dura hasta ahora. Clave: *just, already, yet, ever, never, **since** (punto), **for** (duración)*.

- Estructura: have/has + participio pasado

> *I have worked here since 2022.*
> *They haven't finished the final version yet.*
> *She has worked in cybersecurity since 2019.*

---

### 1.4 Past Simple
**Uso:** acción completada en el pasado, momento específico. Clave: *yesterday, last week, in 2019, ago, when*.

- Regular: verbo + **-ed** | Irregulares: 2ª columna
- Negativa: didn't + infinitivo

> *She wrote the report yesterday.*
> *We went to the cinema last night.*

---

### 1.5 Past Continuous
**Uso:** acción en progreso en el pasado, interrumpida por otra (Past Simple). Clave: *while, when, as*.

- Estructura: was/were + verbo-**ing**

> *We were having dinner when the client called.*

---

### 1.6 Past Perfect Simple
**Uso:** acción ocurrida **antes de otra acción pasada**. Clave: *by the time, before, after, already, when*.

- Estructura: **had** + participio pasado

> *By the time the meeting started, the manager had checked all the reports.*
> *The administrator had fixed the problem before we arrived.*
> *She said that she had sent the email the day before.*

---

### 1.7 Future – WILL
**Uso:** decisión espontánea, promesa, predicción basada en opinión ("I think…"). Clave: *I think, probably, tomorrow, soon*.

- Estructura: will + infinitivo

> *I think the new patch will solve compatibility issues.*
> *If the system crashes again, we will check the logs.*

---

### 1.8 Future – BE GOING TO
**Uso:** plan decidido previamente, predicción con evidencia visible. Clave: *look! / evidencia visual, planes ya decididos*.

- Estructura: am/is/are + **going to** + infinitivo

> *Look at those black clouds, it is going to snow.*
> *We are going to start a new project next month (everything is ready).*

---

### 1.9 Future Continuous
**Uso:** acción **en progreso** en un momento específico del futuro. Clave: *at this time tomorrow, at 10 am tomorrow*.

- Estructura: will be + verbo-**ing**

> *At 10 am tomorrow, we will be taking our networking exam.*
> *At this time tomorrow, we will be testing the new software.*

---

### 1.10 Future Perfect
**Uso:** acción que **habrá terminado** antes de un momento futuro. Clave: **by** + tiempo límite.

- Estructura: will have + participio pasado

> *By next week, the IT team will have completed the software update.*
> *By next semester, we will have studied the whole course.*
> *By next month, they will have opened the new offices.*

---

### RESUMEN RÁPIDO DE TIEMPOS

| Señal clave | Tiempo correcto |
|---|---|
| *usually, every day, always* | Present Simple |
| *now, at the moment, look!* | Present Continuous |
| *since / for* + estado actual | Present Perfect |
| *yesterday, last week, ago* | Past Simple |
| *while / when* (acción en progreso) | Past Continuous |
| *by the time, before* (2 pasados) | Past Perfect |
| *I think, probably* | Future Simple (will) |
| Evidencia visual / plan preparado | Be going to |
| *at this time tomorrow* | Future Continuous |
| **by** + fecha futura | Future Perfect |

## 2. Verbos modales

### 2.1 Obligación y necesidad

| Modal | Uso | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| **must** | Obligación interna / deducción segura presente | *She must be in the office (lights are on).* |
| **have to** | Obligación externa (reglas, normas) | *Students have to wear a uniform.* |
| **mustn't** | Prohibición | *You mustn't share your password.* |
| **don't have to / needn't** | No es necesario | *You don't have to come.* |
| **should** | Consejo / recomendación | *You should use a strong password.* |

> *Employees must wear their ID card at all times.*
> *You must use a strong password to protect your account.*

---

### 2.2 Modal Perfect (pasado con modales)

| Forma | Uso | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| **must have** + participio | Certeza en el pasado | *You must have deleted it by mistake.* |
| **might/may have** + participio | Posibilidad en el pasado | *She might have forgotten.* |
| **could have** + participio | Posibilidad/habilidad no realizada | *They could have fixed it.* |
| **should have** + participio | Crítica / arrepentimiento | *You should have told me.* |

> *The file isn't in the folder. You must have deleted it by mistake.*

## 3. Condicionales y Wish

### Condicionales

| Tipo | Estructura | Uso | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Zero** | If + presente, presente | Verdad universal | *If you heat ice, it melts.* |
| **First** | If + presente, **will** + inf | Situación real/posible | *If the server crashes, we will use backup.* |
| **Second** | If + pasado simple, **would** + inf | Hipotético presente/futuro | *If I had more time, I would learn another language.* |
| **Third** | If + past perfect, **would have** + pp | Hipotético pasado (irreal) | *If they had driven carefully, they would have avoided the accident.* |

**OJO – 2nd conditional:** se usa **were** para todas las personas (no *was*).
> *If I were rich… / If she were here…*

---

### Wish

| Estructura | Uso | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| wish + **past simple** | Deseo presente irreal | *I wish I had more experience.* |
| wish + **past perfect** | Arrepentimiento pasado | *I wish I had studied more.* |
| wish + **would** + inf | Deseo de cambio futuro | *I wish it would stop raining.* |

> *I wish I had more experience in programming.* ← presente irreal

## 4. Voz pasiva

**Estructura:** Sujeto + **verbo to be** (tiempo correspondiente) + **participio pasado** + (by + agente)

| Tiempo | Estructura pasiva | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| Present Simple | is/are + pp | *The software is tested.* |
| Past Simple | was/were + pp | *The bug was fixed.* |
| Future Simple | will be + pp | *The application will be installed next week.* |
| Present Perfect | has/have been + pp | *The bug has been fixed.* |
| Past Perfect | had been + pp | *The meeting room had been prepared.* |
| Modal | must/should be + pp | *The report must be finished by Friday.* |

> *The application will be installed by the IT department next week.*
> *The report must be finished by Friday.*

## 5. Oraciones de relativo

| Pronombre | Uso | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| **who** | Personas (sujeto) | *The technician who repaired the server is on holiday.* |
| **which** | Cosas / animales | *This is the company which developed the security system.* |
| **whose** | Posesión | *The software engineer whose office is next to mine.* |
| **where** | Lugar | *The lab where we test software is secure.* |
| **whom** | Personas (objeto, formal) | *My colleague, whom I worked with, retired.* |

> **OJO:** *that* sólo en defining clauses, nunca en non-defining (con comas).

## 6. Reported Speech

**Regla general:** el tiempo retrocede un paso al reportar.

| Directo | Indirecto |
|---|---|
| *"I sent it"* | She said that she **had sent** it. |
| *"Can you help?"* | He asked me **if** I could help him. |
| *"Don't be late"* | The manager told us **not to** be late. |

> *She said that she had sent the email the day before.*
> *He asked me if I could help him.*
> *The manager told us not to be late.*

## 7. Comparativos y superlativos

| Tipo | Regla | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| 1 sílaba | + er / + est | *fast → faster → fastest* |
| 2+ sílabas | more / most | *efficient → more efficient → most efficient* |
| Irregulares | — | *good/better/best, bad/worse/worst* |

> **Error típico:** ~~more fast~~ → **faster**
> *This laptop is faster than the previous model.*
> *She is the most efficient employee in the department.*
> *The new processor is more efficient than the previous one.*

## 8. Preposiciones

### 8.1 Preposiciones de tiempo

| Preposición | Uso | Ejemplos |
|---|---|---|
| **AT** | Horas exactas, midnight, Easter, breakfast/lunch time, the weekend | *at midnight, at 5 PM, at Easter, at the weekend* |
| **ON** | Días, fechas concretas, días + parte del día | *on Friday, on 24th August, on Tuesday afternoon* |
| **IN** | Meses, años, décadas, estaciones, partes del día | *in June, in 1998, in the nineties, in spring, in the morning* |
| **No preposición** | today, next, this, every + período | *next Saturday, this afternoon, today, every day* |
| **UNTIL** | Hasta un límite temporal | *I worked until 9 PM.* |
| **SINCE** | Desde un punto de inicio | *since 2019, since January* |
| **FOR** | Durante un período | *for many years, for 3 hours* |

> *Mobile phones became popular in the nineties.*
> *She plays tennis on Fridays.*
> *She has worked in cybersecurity since 2019.*
> *The company has been successful for many years.*

---

### 8.2 Preposiciones de lugar

| Preposición | Uso | Ejemplos |
|---|---|---|
| **AT** | Punto concreto, edificios, eventos, addresses | *at the entrance, at work, at the airport, at the traffic lights, at the station, at the bottom of the page* |
| **ON** | Superficie, radio/TV/Internet, transporte público (bus/train/plane), dispositivos | *on the bookshelf, on the radio, on the plane, on the fridge, on her T-shirt, on the bus* |
| **IN** | Interior (espacio cerrado), ciudades, países, taxi/car | *in the box, in Germany, in London, in a taxi, in bed, in the house* |
| **BY** | Al lado de, junto a | *by the river, by the window* |

> *The firewall is located at the entrance of the room.*
> *She was listening to music on the radio.*
> *Berlin is in Germany.*
> *He has a house by the river.*
> *John is in a taxi.*

## 9. Conectores

| Conector | Función | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| **because** | Causa (+ oración) | *She stayed late because she had work.* |
| **because of** | Causa (+ sustantivo/frase nominal) | *The office was closed because of the public holiday.* |
| **although / even though** | Contraste | *Although the problem was serious, they solved it.* |
| **so** | Resultado/consecuencia | *She works hard, so she often stays late.* |
| **however** | Contraste (entre oraciones) | *It was expensive; however, we bought it.* |

> **OJO:** *because of* va seguido de sustantivo, no de oración.
> *The office was closed because of the public holiday.* ✓
> ~~because of it rained~~ ✗

## 10. Gerundios e infinitivos tras verbos

Algunos verbos imponen una estructura fija:

| Estructura | Verbos | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| verbo + **-ing** | deny, avoid, finish, admit | *He denied copying the files.* |
| look forward to + **-ing** | (expresión fija) | *She is looking forward to attending the course.* |
| verbo + **not to** + infinitivo (reported) | tell, ask, order | *The manager told us not to be late.* |

> *He denied copying the confidential files.*
> *She is looking forward to attending the training course.*

## 14. Trampas del examen

### Errores más comunes

**Will vs Be going to**
- *"I think it will rain"* → will (predicción/opinión sin evidencia)
- *"Look at those clouds, it's going to rain"* → going to (evidencia visible)
- *"We are going to start next month, everything is prepared"* → going to (plan decidido)

**Present Perfect vs Past Simple**
- *"I have worked here since 2022"* → Present Perfect (estado que dura)
- *"She wrote the report yesterday"* → Past Simple (momento concreto)

**Since vs For**
- **since** + punto de inicio: *since 2019, since January*
- **for** + duración: *for many years, for 3 hours*

**Future Perfect vs Future Simple**
- "By next week" → **will have + pp** (Future Perfect)
- "I think tomorrow" → **will + infinitivo**

**Modales pasados**
- *"The file isn't here"* → **must have deleted** (certeza pasada)
- Nunca *"must deleted"* ni *"must has deleted"*

**Passive voice**
- Modal + pasiva: **must/will + be + pp** → *must be finished, will be installed*
- Nunca *"must finished"* ni *"will installed"*

**Relative pronouns**
- **who** → solo personas
- **which** → solo cosas
- **whose** → posesión (de quien sea)
- **where** → lugar
- **that** → solo en defining clauses (sin comas), nunca non-defining

**Comparativos**
- 1 sílaba: **faster** (nunca *more fast*)
- 2+ sílabas: **more efficient** (nunca *efficienter*)

**Reported speech**
- "Don't be late" → told us **not to** be late (no *"don't"*)
- "I sent it" → said she **had sent** it (backshift)

**Preposiciones trampa**
- *at the weekend* (no *on the weekend*)
- *in the morning/afternoon/evening* pero *at night*
- *on the radio/TV/plane* pero *in a car/taxi*
- *at the entrance/station/airport* (punto concreto)
- *today, next Saturday, this afternoon* → **sin preposición**

**Gerundios fijos**
- *look forward to* + **-ing** (no infinitivo)
- *deny* + **-ing** (no infinitivo)

## MINI-CHULETA FINAL

```
TENSES:
  rutina          → Present Simple       (verb+s/es; do/does + inf)
  ahora           → Present Continuous   (am/is/are + verb-ing)
  since/for/yet   → Present Perfect      (have/has + pp)
  ayer/last/ago   → Past Simple          (verb-ed / 2ª columna; did + inf)
  while/as        → Past Continuous      (was/were + verb-ing)
  by the time     → Past Perfect         (had + pp)
  I think/prob.   → Future Simple        (will + inf)
  evidencia/plan  → be going to          (am/is/are + going to + inf)
  at this time ↑  → Future Continuous    (will be + verb-ing)
  by + futuro     → Future Perfect       (will have + pp)

MODALS:
  must            → obligación / deducción segura presente
  must have + pp  → certeza pasada
  have to         → obligación externa (reglas)
  may/might       → posibilidad

CONDITIONALS:
  0: present → present
  1: present → will + inf
  2: past simple → would + inf  (were para todas personas)
  3: past perfect → would have + pp

PASSIVE: be (tiempo) + participio pasado
RELATIVE: who=persona | which=cosa | whose=posesión | where=lugar

PREPOSITIONS tiempo:
  AT: horas, midnight, Easter, breakfast, weekend
  ON: días, fechas
  IN: meses, años, décadas, estaciones, morning/afternoon/evening
  Ø:  today, next, this + período

PREPOSITIONS lugar:
  AT: punto concreto (entrance, work, airport, station)
  ON: superficie, radio, plane, fridge, T-shirt
  IN: interior, ciudad, país, car/taxi
  BY: al lado de (river, window)

VOCAB IT: compile, debug, toggle, breakpoint, watchpoint,
          software/hardware, web browser, WAP, number cruncher
```
