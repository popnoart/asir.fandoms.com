## T1-U1: PRESENT TENSES

- **Present Simple**: rutinas, hechos, horarios. *Siempre, nunca, cada día.*  
- **Present Continuous**: ahora mismo, temporal, futuro fijo. *Now, at the moment.*  
- **Present Perfect Simple**: experiencias, resultados presentes, *just, already, yet, ever, never, for, since.*  
- **Present Perfect Continuous**: duración desde el pasado hasta ahora. *For, since, all day.*  
- **Contables vs incontables** \+ *many/much, a few/a little, some/any.*

| Tiempo | Uso clave | Palabras clave | Ejemplo |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Present Simple** | Rutinas, verdades universales, horarios | *always, usually, often, sometimes, never, every day* | *She works at Google.* |
| **Present Continuous** | Ahora mismo, temporal, planes fijos | *now, at the moment, right now, today, this week* | *I'm reading a book.* |
| **Present Perfect Simple** | Experiencia, resultado presente, acción reciente | *just, already, yet, ever, never, for, since, today* | *She has written a book.* |
| **Present Perfect Continuous** | Énfasis en duración (acción que empezó y sigue) | *for, since, all day, how long, lately, recently* | *He has been surfing for 7 hours.* |

## T1-U2: PAST TENSES

- **Past Simple**: acción completa y puntual. *Yesterday, last week, ago.*  
- **Past Continuous**: acción en progreso interrumpida. *While, when.*  
- **Used to**: hábitos o estados pasados que ya no son ciertos.  
- **Past Perfect Simple**: acción anterior a otra pasada. *After, before, by the time.*  
- **Past Perfect Continuous**: duración de una acción antes de otra pasada.  
- **Doble consonante**: *sit → sitting* (1 vocal \+ 1 consonante, sílaba tónica).  
- **Direcciones y phrasal verbs de viajes**: *set off, hold up, see off, check in/out, stop over.*

| Tiempo | Uso clave | Palabras clave | Ejemplo |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Past Simple** | Acción completada en pasado | *yesterday, last week, in 2010, ... ago, when* | *I visited Paris.* |
| **Past Continuous** | En progreso (interrumpida o simultánea) | *while, when, as, this time last year* | *I was reading when you called.* |
| **Used to** | Hábito pasado (ya no) | *(sin palabras clave específicas)* | *I used to play soccer.* |
| **Past Perfect Simple** | Antes de otra acción pasada | *after, before, by the time, as soon as, until, already, just, never* | *She had already left when I arrived.* |
| **Past Perfect Continuous** | Duración antes de otra acción pasada | *how long, before, since, for, all day, until* | *They had been working for hours before the crash.* |

## T1-U3: PERFECT TENSES (repaso)

- **Just, already, yet, ever, never, for, since** (clave para perfectos).  
- **Preposiciones de lugar**: *at* (punto), *on* (superficie/transporte), *in* (espacio/ciudad).  
- **Preposiciones de tiempo**: *on* (días), *in* (meses/años), *at* (horas/festividades).

- ## 

| Expresión | Uso | Ejemplo |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Just** | Acaba de ocurrir | *I have just finished.* |
| **Already** | Antes de lo esperado | *She has already arrived.* |
| **Yet** | No ha ocurrido (neg/interrog) | *Have you eaten yet?* |
| **Ever** | En algún momento (preguntas) | *Have you ever been to Paris?* |
| **Never** | Nunca en la vida | *I have never seen that.* |
| **For** | Duración (periodo de tiempo) | *for 5 years* |
| **Since** | Punto de inicio | *since 2020* |

## T1-U4: FUTURE TENSES

- **Will**: decisiones espontáneas, promesas, predicciones sin evidencia.  
- **Be going to**: planes previos, evidencia visible.  
- **Present Continuous with future**: acuerdos fijos (hora/lugar).  
- **Future Continuous**: en progreso en un momento futuro.  
- **Future Perfect**: completado antes de un momento futuro (*by \+ tiempo*).  
- **Conectores**: adición (and), contraste (but), causa/efecto (because), tiempo (then).

| Tiempo | Uso clave | Palabras clave | Ejemplo |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Will** | Decisión espontánea, predicción, promesa | *tomorrow, next week, in 2030, soon, later* | *I will help you.* |
| **Be going to** | Plan previo, evidencia visible | *tomorrow, next week, this evening, soon, look\!* | *It's going to rain.* |
| **Present Continuous** (futuro) | Acuerdo fijo (hora, lugar, persona) | *tomorrow, next week, this evening, tonight, on Friday* | *I'm meeting John tonight.* |
| **Future Continuous** | En progreso en momento futuro | *this time tomorrow, at 8 PM tomorrow, by then, in \[x\] hours* | *I will be working at 8 PM.* |
| **Future Perfect** | Completado antes de momento futuro | *by \[specific time\], by the time, by then, before* | *I will have finished by 5 PM.* |

## T2-U5: MODAL TENSES

- **Can/Could/Be able to**: habilidad.  
- **May/Might/Must/Can’t**: posibilidad y deducción.  
- **Must/Have to/Should/Ought to**: obligación y consejo.  
- **Modal Perfect**: *must have been, might have forgotten, should have told me.*  
- **Adverbios**: modo, lugar, tiempo, frecuencia, grado.  
- **Adverbios de frecuencia:** always, never, often, sometimes, usually (van ANTES del verbo principal)  
- **Comparativos y superlativos**: reglas cortos/largos(tall→taller, big→bigger, amazing→more amazing), irregulares (*good → better → best*), igualdad (*as...as*), inferioridad (*less...than*).

| Grupo | Modales | Palabras clave (implícitas) | Ejemplo |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Habilidad/Permiso** | can, could, be able to, be allowed to | (contexto de capacidad o permiso) | *I can swim.* |
| **Posibilidad/Deducción** | may (70%), might (30%), must (seguro), can't (imposible) | *maybe, perhaps, I'm sure, I'm certain* | *It might rain.* |
| **Obligación/Consejo** | must, have to, mustn't, should | *necessary, important, optional, forbidden* | *You should see a doctor.* |

## T2-U6: CONDITIONAL TENSES

- **Zero**: verdades universales (*if \+ presente, presente*).  
- **First**: posible futuro (*if \+ presente, will*).  
- **Second**: hipotético presente/futuro (*if \+ pasado, would*).  
- **Third**: hipotético pasado (*if \+ past perfect, would have \+ participio*).  
- **Wish clauses**:  
  - Presente: *wish \+ pasado simple*  
  - Pasado: *wish \+ past perfect*  
  - Futuro: *wish \+ would \+ infinitivo*  
- **CV y emails formales**: expresiones clave (*I am writing to inform you...*, *yours sincerely/faithfully*).

| Tipo | If clause | Main clause | Palabras clave | Ejemplo |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Zero** | presente | presente | *when, whenever, if* | *If you heat ice, it melts.* |
| **1st** | presente | will \+ inf | *if, unless, as soon as, when, before, after* | *If it rains, we will stay home.* |
| **2nd** | past simple | would \+ inf | *if, if I were you* | *If I were rich, I would travel.* |
| **3rd** | past perfect | would have \+ pp | *if, if only* | *If I had studied, I would have passed.* |

## T2-U7: PASSIVE VOICE

- **Estructura**: sujeto \+ *to be \+ participio pasado* (mismo tiempo que la activa).  
- **Uso de “get”** en pasiva informal (*got stolen*).  
- **Causativos**:  
  - *Have someone do something*  
  - *Get someone to do something*  
  - *Make someone do something* (obligar)  
  - *Let someone do something* (permitir)  
- **Causativo de servicio**: *have/get \+ objeto \+ participio* (*have my hair cut*).  
- **Phrasal verbs laborales**: *apply for, go through, follow up, take on, turn down, show up.*  
- **Preguntas típicas de entrevista**.

| Tiempo | Activa | Pasiva |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Presente simple | *The team tests the software.* | *The software is tested.* |
| Pasado simple | *The thief stole the car.* | *The car was stolen.* |
| Futuro simple | *They will install the update.* | *The update will be installed.* |
| Presente perfecto | *Someone has fixed the bug.* | *The bug has been fixed.* |

## T3-U8: RELATIVE CLAUSES

-  **Pronombres:** who (personas), which (cosas), that (defining), whose (posesión), where (lugar), when (tiempo)  
- **Defining** (sin comas, información necesaria): *who, which, that, whose, where, when.*  
- **Non-defining** (con comas, info extra): *who, which, whose, where* (no *that*).  
- **Pronombres con preposiciones**: formal (*to which*) vs informal (*which...to*).  
- **Phrasal verbs técnicos**: *back into, boot off, hack on, link on, log up, log off.*

| Tipo | Comas | Información | Pronombres | ¿Se puede omitir? |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Defining** | No | Esencial | who, which, that, whose, where | ❌ No |
| **Non-defining** | Sí | Adicional | who, which, whose, where (no *that*) | ✅ Sí |
